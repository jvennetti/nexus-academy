## Lesson 9 — Stop Wasting Your Claude Tokens. Do This Instead...
**~varies**

▶ [Watch Now](https://youtu.be/riN4S9AoaB0)

⚠️ *New addition — watch this before the prompt engineering deep dive. Token awareness is foundational to everything that follows.*

Most people don't realize their Claude output degrades the longer a conversation runs — and it's not random. There's a mechanical reason it happens, and a handful of habits that fix it. This video covers both.

**The core concept it introduces:** Context management — controlling how much Claude is holding in its working memory at any given time. It's the main difference between people who get consistently great output and people who feel like it's a coin flip.

**Key takeaways:**
- Every message Claude receives, it re-reads the entire conversation from the beginning. Costs compound — they don't add.
- Think of the context window like short-term memory. When it fills, output quality drops and early instructions get deprioritized.
- Start fresh conversations intentionally. Long, sprawling threads are one of the most common reasons Claude degrades mid-session.
- Using Projects with well-structured knowledge files is the core fix — context that lives in the project doesn't eat your conversation window.

## Lesson 10 — AI Prompt Engineering: A Deep Dive
**Anthropic | ~1 hr 16 min**

▶ [Watch Now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9aRN5JkmL8)

This is the backbone of the module. Anthropic's own prompt team — the people who built Claude — sitting down to talk through what prompt engineering actually is, what moves output quality, and where most people go wrong. Treat it like a masterclass, because it is one.

Split this into two viewing blocks. Don't try to absorb 76 minutes in one sitting. Watch the first half, take notes or test a few prompts inside Claude, then come back and finish the second half.

**Key takeaways:**
- Prompt engineering is clear communication — the same principles that make human instructions work make prompts work
- Context, role, format, and examples are the four levers that move output quality more than anything else
- The people in this video built the model — their framing of what prompting is (and isn't) is the closest thing to a ground truth you'll find
- Iteration is the method. A first prompt is a hypothesis. You refine it the same way you'd refine any professional communication.
- System prompts (used in Projects) are fundamentally different from conversational prompts — understand the distinction early.

## Module 2 Challenge

**The Real-World Scenario:**

You've just landed your first AI consulting engagement. Your client is a boutique financial advisory firm with 12 employees. They want to use AI to improve how they prepare for client meetings — currently a manual, time-consuming process involving research, agenda drafting, and note-taking. They've asked you to show them what that could look like.

**Your challenge:**

Set up a dedicated Claude Project for this client. Write the system prompt that will anchor every interaction in that project — defining the firm's context, the kinds of tasks Claude will handle, the tone, and any constraints. Then write a prompt that generates a structured client meeting prep document for a fictional client: a 55-year-old business owner looking to discuss retirement planning options.

Your deliverable is a working Claude Project with a system prompt, plus a completed meeting prep document Claude generated from it. Screenshot or export both. This is the proof of concept you'd hand to a client after your first working session.

> **Integration note:** Financial Services is one of the highest-leverage AI adoption areas — meeting prep, compliance documentation, client communication, advisor research. You now have the prompting foundation to start building real things in that space.
