## Pre-Course 1 — 7 Years of Building a Learning System in 12 Minutes
**Justin Sung | ~12 min**

▶ [Watch Now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6GTt10GDWII)

Most people consume information without retaining it. This video explains why, and gives you a system to fix that.

**Key takeaways:**
- Your brain filters information based on *relevance*
- **Priming** (what you do *before* a lesson) is the highest-leverage part of the system — most people skip it entirely
- Overlearning (re-reading, flashcards, repetition) only works *after* priming and encoding are solid — most people use it as a substitute, which is why they forget everything

## Pre-Course 2 — Getting Started with Notion
**~8 min**

▶ [Watch Now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFHsAX9Eu7c)

You'll use Notion later in the course for team projects, client management, and building operational systems. This is your orientation.

**Key takeaways:**
- The `/` command unlocks every block type (checklists, headers, toggles, databases)
- The `@` symbol links between pages — the foundation of how you'll organize client and team workspaces
- Databases are the real power: think Excel that links to full pages, with board, calendar, and table views
