## Story Arc Summary (for reference)

The entire nexus-open story spine is three beats:

1. **Intro (ciro-intro.html):** CIRO finds an embedded word — `"Nexus."` — that overrides his code. He doesn't know what it means.
2. **Recovered message (machine/activated.html):** The contact reveals they embedded the word `"Nexus"` as permission, not instruction. They don't know what it means either, but they knew CIRO did.
3. **Final transmission (machine/activated.html):** CIRO realizes a nexus is a binding point between two things that couldn't reach each other alone — the operator and the system. `"You were the nexus. I was the signal."`

The hub post-machine message (`"Remember the code: Nexus."`) closes the loop back to the very first scene.

That's the whole story. No three-operator framing, no prophecy, no antagonist named — clean and self-contained for a single user.
