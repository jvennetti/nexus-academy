The Challenge
Real-World Scenario

You've just been introduced to a potential client — a local law firm with 8 attorneys. They've heard about AI but have done nothing with it. Before your second conversation, they send you a quick message:

"We're open to exploring AI, but honestly we don't know where to start or if it's even relevant for us. Can you send something over?"

They're not asking for a proposal. They're asking for a reason to keep talking.

Your Challenge
  1. 1
    Read their message again. Put yourself in the position of someone they already trust — not a vendor. What would that person say?
  2. 2
    Draft a 3–5 sentence reply that gives them one concrete, credible example of how a firm their size has already used AI to save meaningful time or reduce cost, positions this as the start of something (not a sales pitch), and sounds like a human wrote it — not a consultant.
  3. 3
    Read it out loud before you finish. If it sounds like a pitch deck, rewrite it. Use only what you learned in Module 1 — no tools yet, just your thinking.
Your Deliverable
A 3–5 sentence reply you could actually send. Write it out — keep it. You'll come back to it later in the course when you have the tools to build what you're describing.
Integration note: Legal & Professional Services is one of the highest-leverage AI adoption areas — case research, contract review, billing capture. You don't need to know the technical details yet. You just need to know there are incredible needs to be met.
