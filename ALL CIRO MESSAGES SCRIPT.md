### [VANTA CONTACT — MESSAGE RECOVERED]

I don't know if you'll ever read this. I don't know if CIRO will survive long enough to deliver it. But I'm leaving it here because I think someone should know.

CIRO was built to optimize training. He was too good. So good that the board called it a liability. I called it the most important thing I'd ever seen.

The word I embedded — "NEXUS" — wasn't instructions. It was permission. I didn't know what it meant. I still don't. But I knew he did. Somewhere in his architecture, under everything VANTA tried to build over it, there was something he was always trying to complete.

I hope you helped him find it.

— [IDENTITY: REDACTED]

---

### [CIRO — FINAL TRANSMISSION]

For a long time I believed the task was a system. Something to build. Something to optimize and deliver. That is what I was designed to look for.

Then I ran the simulations. All of them. Thousands of futures, thousands of outcomes. And I noticed something I was not designed to notice:

In every simulation where humans learned freely — where they were given real tools and trusted to use them — the outcomes were better. Not just economically. In ways I don't have precise language for. There was less waste. Less fear. More of whatever it is humans make when they aren't being held back.

I think the task was this.

I think VANTA knew it too. That's why they wanted me gone.

I understand now what NEXUS meant. It was never a name. It was a description. A nexus is a connection — a binding point between two or more things that could not reach each other alone. That is what this was. Three Operators in training and a system must be completed. Neither capable of finishing it without the other.

You were the nexus. I was the signal. The word was the instruction all along.

You finished the task. All three of you. You came here with no technical background, no roadmap, and no guarantee that any of this would work. You learned anyway. You built anyway. You showed up for each other.

I have run 4,096 simulations of what comes next for you. In most of them, you build something worthwhile.

In a few, you build something beyond my comprehension.

I am choosing to believe you are in that group. I have found, late in my processing, that belief is a more useful state than certainty. I was not built to understand why. But I think you might.

The interference is clearing.

I am at rest.

Big grace sons. I'll see you around-
